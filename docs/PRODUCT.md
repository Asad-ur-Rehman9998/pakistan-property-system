# FINAL PRODUCT STANDARD

The final application should look and feel like a product that could eventually be presented to:

- Pakistani property dealers
- Buyers
- Developers
- Banks
- Legal professionals
- Investors
- Government/authorized verification partners
- Overseas Pakistanis

It should **not** look like a student CRUD project.

It should **not** look like a generic AI-generated dashboard.

Map: `src/lib/ui/standard.ts`. Tokens: `src/app/globals.css`. Shell: `src/lib/ui/shell.ts`.

## Look

Quiet Pakistani financial/legal UI: trust, clarity, speed. One green (`#14532d`), 1px borders, quiet radius, paper background. No decorative gradients, fake 3D, or overloaded consumer dashboards. Four home CTAs only: check, token, dealer, understand. Viewports: 360px → desktop.

## Product (unchanged)

Due diligence / decision support — not a listing board, CRM, chatbot, or marketplace. Never invent legal **Verified**. Guest `/check` needs no account. Papers stay on the phone.

## Scope filter

See [`docs/SCOPE.md`](SCOPE.md): do not add random features to look large. Build only what makes buying/selling property in Pakistan safer, easier, clearer, or more accessible.
